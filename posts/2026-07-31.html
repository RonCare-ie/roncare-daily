UK Launches Cross-Government Action Plan: Millions of Unpaid Carers to Receive Better Recognition and Support

Government publishes coordinated strategy on 14 July 2026 to identify carers early, refer them to support, and help reach their full potential.

Right now, Ireland's 624,000 family carers are carrying more than anyone should carry alone, according to the State of Caring 2026 report by Family Carers Ireland. The same report found that 71% of family carers are struggling to make ends meet, and 75% have never received a single break through respite care. On 8 July 2026, Family Carers Ireland called on Government to use Budget 2027 to close the gap between political promises and the daily reality carers face. It makes knowing your entitlements in Ireland more important than ever.

The biggest recent change for Irish carers is one you'll want to check today. From 2 July 2026, the Department of Social Protection introduced the largest-ever increase to the Carer's Allowance means test: the weekly income disregard jumped from €625 to €1,000 for a single carer, and from €1,250 to €2,000 for a couple. That means a two-adult household can now earn significantly more and still qualify. The current weekly rate of Carer's Allowance is €270 if you're under 66, or €308 if you're 66 or over — and if you care for two or more people, you may receive up to €405 per week. You can check your eligibility and apply online through MyWelfare, or get a personalised estimate by calling Citizens Information on 0761 07 4000 before you apply.

On top of Carer's Allowance, there are two other supports that carers in Ireland often don't know they're entitled to. The Carer's Support Grant is a €2,000 annual lump sum paid each June — it's not means-tested, it's completely tax-free, and it's paid for each person you care for, so if you're caring for two people, you can receive €4,000. Over 147,000 carers received it on 4 June 2026. If you're already on Carer's Allowance, Carer's Benefit, or Domiciliary Care Allowance, you get it automatically — no form needed. If you're not on any of those payments but you provide full-time care, you can still apply using form CSG1, available from citizensinformation.ie.

If the person you care for needs practical help at home — personal care, help with meals or mobility — the HSE Home Support Service is free and not means-tested. You don't even need a medical card to apply. To request an assessment, fill in an application form and return it to your local HSE Home Support Office — you can find yours at hse.ie/homesupportservice, or call HSE Live free on 1800 700 700. Your GP, a public health nurse, or a hospital discharge coordinator can also help you request an assessment and submit the form on your behalf. One thing to know: there is currently no legal right to home support in Ireland — it's a discretionary service based on assessed need and local availability. The Health (Amendment) (Home Support Providers) Bill 2025, passed in June 2026, will introduce formal regulation and minimum standards for all home support providers across the country.

If you're caring for a loved one right now, call Citizens Information on 0761 07 4000 today. Whether you've never applied for anything before, had a payment reduced, or were turned down for Carer's Allowance before July 2026, the new income rules mean your situation may have changed. You deserve every euro of support that's available to you.
